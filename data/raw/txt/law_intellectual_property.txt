Intellectual Property

Intellectual property refers to legal rights that protect creations of the mind. Major categories include patents, copyrights, trademarks, and trade secrets. A patent protects certain inventions for a limited period in exchange for public disclosure. Copyright protects original works such as books, music, software, art, and films. Trademarks protect signs that identify the source of goods or services.

Trade secrets protect valuable confidential information such as formulas, processes, or business methods. Intellectual property law aims to encourage innovation and creativity while balancing public access. The scope and duration of rights vary by category and jurisdiction. Infringement occurs when someone uses protected intellectual property without permission in a way that violates the owner's rights.
