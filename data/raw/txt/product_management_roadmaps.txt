Product Roadmaps

A product roadmap is a strategic plan that communicates the direction, priorities, and intended outcomes of a product over time. Roadmaps help teams align around customer problems, business goals, and delivery sequencing. A roadmap may include themes, initiatives, features, milestones, or experiments depending on the organization.

Good roadmaps explain why work matters, not just what will be built. They should be updated as teams learn from users, market changes, technical constraints, and data. A roadmap is not a fixed promise of exact dates unless the organization treats it that way. Product managers use roadmaps to coordinate engineering, design, sales, support, marketing, and leadership. Outcome-focused roadmaps emphasize measurable customer or business results rather than lists of features alone.
