Sustainable Building Design

Sustainable building design aims to reduce environmental impact while creating healthy and functional spaces. It considers energy use, water consumption, materials, indoor air quality, site selection, waste reduction, and long-term maintenance. Passive design strategies use building orientation, insulation, shading, natural ventilation, and daylight to reduce heating, cooling, and lighting demand.

Efficient mechanical systems, solar panels, rainwater harvesting, green roofs, and low-impact materials can further improve performance. Life-cycle thinking evaluates environmental impacts from material extraction through construction, operation, and demolition. Sustainable buildings are not only about technology; occupant behavior, maintenance, and local climate also affect actual performance. Good design balances environmental goals with safety, comfort, cost, and cultural context.
