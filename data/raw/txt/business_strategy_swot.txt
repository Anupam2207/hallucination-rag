SWOT Analysis

SWOT analysis is a planning framework that examines strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Strengths and weaknesses are usually internal factors, such as capabilities, resources, brand, skills, or operational limits. Opportunities and threats are external factors, such as market trends, competitors, regulation, technology, or customer behavior.

Organizations use SWOT analysis to structure strategic thinking and identify actions. A strength can be used to pursue an opportunity, while a weakness may need investment or mitigation. SWOT is simple and useful for discussion, but it can become superficial if not supported by evidence. Strong analysis connects each point to data, customer insight, financial impact, or competitive context. SWOT should lead to priorities rather than remain a static list.
