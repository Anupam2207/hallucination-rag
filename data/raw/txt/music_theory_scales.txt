Musical Scales

A musical scale is an ordered set of pitches used as the basis for melodies and harmonies. In Western music, the major scale has a pattern of whole and half steps that produces a bright and stable sound. The natural minor scale uses a different step pattern and often sounds darker or more somber. Other scale types include pentatonic, chromatic, harmonic minor, melodic minor, and modes.

Scales help musicians organize pitch relationships and create expectations. The tonic is the central note of a scale, and other notes are heard in relation to it. Chords are often built by stacking notes from a scale. Scales are used in composition, improvisation, ear training, and analysis. Different musical traditions use different tuning systems and scale structures.
