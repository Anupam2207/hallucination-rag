Urbanization

Urbanization is the process by which a growing share of a population lives in towns and cities. It is driven by industrialization, employment opportunities, education, healthcare access, infrastructure, and migration from rural areas. Cities can provide economic opportunity and efficient service delivery, but rapid urban growth can strain housing, transportation, sanitation, and public services.

Urbanization changes social relationships, land use, labor markets, and environmental conditions. Informal settlements may expand when housing supply does not keep pace with population growth. Good urban planning includes transportation networks, affordable housing, water systems, waste management, parks, and climate resilience. Urbanization is not only a demographic shift; it also transforms culture, governance, and economic activity.
