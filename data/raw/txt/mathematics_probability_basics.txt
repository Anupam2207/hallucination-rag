Probability Basics

Probability is the mathematical study of uncertainty. A probability value ranges from 0 to 1, where 0 means an event is impossible and 1 means it is certain. The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes, and an event is a subset of the sample space. For equally likely outcomes, probability can be calculated by dividing favorable outcomes by total outcomes.

Conditional probability measures the chance of an event given that another event has occurred. Independent events do not affect each other's probabilities. Expected value is a weighted average of possible outcomes, using probabilities as weights. Probability theory supports statistics, machine learning, risk analysis, finance, engineering reliability, genetics, and decision-making under uncertainty.
