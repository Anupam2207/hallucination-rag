Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because iron oxide on its surface gives it a reddish appearance. Mars is a rocky planet with volcanoes, valleys, impact craters, polar ice caps, and dust storms.

The Martian atmosphere is thin and mostly carbon dioxide. Because the atmosphere is thin, liquid water is not stable on the surface under most present-day conditions. Evidence from orbiters and rovers suggests that ancient Mars had flowing water in some regions.

Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. Olympus Mons on Mars is one of the largest volcanoes in the Solar System. Valles Marineris is a vast canyon system much larger than the Grand Canyon on Earth.

Mars exploration includes orbiters, landers, and rovers. NASA's Curiosity and Perseverance rovers study geology, climate history, and signs of past habitability. Mars is a major target for astrobiology because ancient environments may have been more favorable for microbial life.
