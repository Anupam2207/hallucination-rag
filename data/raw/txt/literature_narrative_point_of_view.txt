Narrative Point of View

Narrative point of view describes the perspective from which a story is told. First-person narration uses pronouns such as I or we and presents events through a character's personal viewpoint. Third-person narration uses pronouns such as he, she, or they. A third-person limited narrator focuses on the thoughts and experiences of one character, while a third-person omniscient narrator can reveal the thoughts of multiple characters.

Point of view shapes what readers know, trust, and feel. An unreliable narrator gives information that may be biased, incomplete, or misleading. Second-person narration addresses the reader as you, but it is less common in fiction. Authors choose point of view to control intimacy, suspense, distance, and interpretation.
