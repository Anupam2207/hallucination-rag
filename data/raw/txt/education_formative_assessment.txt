Formative Assessment

Formative assessment is assessment used during learning to guide instruction and help students improve. It differs from summative assessment, which evaluates learning at the end of a unit or course. Examples of formative assessment include quizzes, exit tickets, classroom questioning, draft feedback, peer review, concept maps, and quick writing prompts.

The purpose of formative assessment is not simply to assign a grade. It helps teachers identify misconceptions, adjust instruction, and provide timely feedback. It helps students understand learning goals and monitor their own progress. Effective formative assessment is specific, actionable, and connected to instruction. Digital tools can make feedback faster, but the value comes from how the evidence of learning is used.
