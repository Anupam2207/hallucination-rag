Nutrition

Nutrition is the study of how food and nutrients affect health, growth, energy, and disease risk. Macronutrients include carbohydrates, fats, and proteins, which provide energy and building blocks. Micronutrients include vitamins and minerals that support processes such as immunity, bone health, blood formation, and nerve function.

A balanced diet typically includes a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein sources, and healthy fats. Dietary needs vary by age, activity level, health status, pregnancy, culture, and personal goals. Malnutrition can involve undernutrition, micronutrient deficiency, or excessive intake of energy-dense foods. Nutrition science studies patterns of eating rather than single foods alone, because overall diet quality matters for long-term health.
