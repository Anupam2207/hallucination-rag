Cultural Diffusion

Cultural diffusion is the spread of ideas, technologies, practices, languages, foods, beliefs, or institutions from one society or group to another. Diffusion can occur through trade, migration, conquest, education, media, travel, and digital communication. Examples include the spread of crops, writing systems, religions, clothing styles, and scientific knowledge.

Cultural diffusion does not mean that a receiving society copies something unchanged. People often adapt outside influences to local needs and values. Diffusion can enrich societies, but it can also create conflict when cultural practices are imposed or commercialized without respect. Anthropologists study diffusion to understand how cultures change, interact, and maintain identity over time.
