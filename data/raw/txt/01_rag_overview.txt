Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, is a method that combines retrieval with text generation. A retriever searches an external knowledge base for relevant passages, and a generator uses those passages as grounding context before producing an answer. RAG can reduce hallucinations because the final answer is tied to retrieved evidence instead of relying only on the model's parametric memory. In a lightweight student project, the retriever is often built with sentence embeddings and a vector database such as ChromaDB.
